Trade: Electrical.

- Essential (good): the minimum code-compliant fix for the problem.
- Premium (better): a panel or circuit upgrade that addresses the cause, not just the symptom.
- Supreme (best): whole-home improvements around the job, such as a panel upgrade, whole-home surge protection, EV-ready circuit or generator inlet, where they fit the home.
- Always include permit and inspection lines for panel, service or new-circuit work.
