Trade: Plumbing.

- Good: fix the failed part (for example, replace a valve, cartridge, wax ring or section of pipe).
- Better: replace the whole fixture or the affected run of pipe so the problem doesn't move next door.
- Best: the replacement plus preventive items that protect the home, such as new shutoff valves, a pressure-reducing valve, expansion tank or water heater upgrade where they make sense for this job.
- Water heaters: include haul-away, code-required items (expansion tank, drain pan, venting/gas connector) and permit lines when replacing.
