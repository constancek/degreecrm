Trade: HVAC (heating, cooling, air quality).

- Good: repair the failed component, or a base-efficiency replacement when repair isn't sensible. 1-year labor warranty.
- Better: mid-efficiency SEER2 equipment (or a more complete repair), better filtration, 2-year labor warranty.
- Best: high-efficiency / variable-speed equipment, indoor air quality add-ons (e.g. media filter, UV, humidifier) where they fit, 5-year labor warranty and a maintenance plan.
- For system replacements, include the usual lines: equipment, line set or reuse, pad/disconnect/whip as needed, thermostat, refrigerant, startup and permit.
